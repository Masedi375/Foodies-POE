OUR STORY

About Foodies

We believe good food should be enjoyable, accessible and easy to make.

Our mission

Foodies was founded by Ofentse Masedi on 9 August 2026 to inspire people to try something different in their day-to-day cooking.

Our mission is to help people fall in love with food, discover diverse cuisines and make healthier choices through approachable recipes and practical cooking guidance.

Who we cook for

Parents & families

Ideas for sharing tasty meals at home.

Students

Inspiration for simple, budget-conscious cooking.

Food enthusiasts

New flavours and recipes to explore.
